# Savings Goals: How They Work in This App

A savings goal lets a customer track progress toward a specific target amount by a target date, linked to one account. Progress is calculated from the linked account's current balance against the goal's target amount.

Common questions:

**How is progress calculated?**
Progress percentage is the linked account's current balance divided by the goal's target amount, capped at 100%. Time remaining is the number of days between today and the target date.

**What do the goal statuses mean?**
- `NOT_STARTED` - the goal was just created and has no meaningful progress yet.
- `IN_PROGRESS` - actively being tracked, before the target date.
- `ACHIEVED` - the linked account balance has reached or passed the target amount.
- `OVERDUE` - the target date has passed without reaching the target amount.

**Can I have more than one goal?**
Each account can have one active savings goal at a time. Multiple accounts can each have their own goal.

**What if I don't have a savings goal set up yet?**
That's fine - general savings and budgeting guidance still applies without one. Setting a specific goal (with a name, target amount, and target date) tends to make saving more concrete and easier to stick to than an open-ended "save more" intention.

**Does closing or freezing an account affect a goal?**
A goal tracks the balance of its linked account, so if that account is frozen or closed, progress tracking against it is affected accordingly. For account-specific issues, checking the account's status directly is the most reliable source.
