# GICs Explained

A Guaranteed Investment Certificate (GIC) is a savings product that locks in your principal for a fixed term.

## Key points

- Your principal is guaranteed at maturity.
- You earn a fixed, guaranteed return over the term.
- Funds are typically locked in until maturity.
- A GIC may suit money you do not need for day-to-day cash flow.

## When a GIC may help

- You want predictable growth over a known term.
- You can keep those funds untouched until maturity.

## When a GIC may not fit

- You may need quick access to the funds.
- You prioritize liquidity over fixed returns.
